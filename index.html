ABOUT US

Shaping Mumbai's Skyline Since 2006

We are a consulting and liaisoning firm that believes every project deserves the right guidance. For two decades, we have stood alongside developers, housing societies and property owners, helping them with architectural design, building approvals and redevelopment with confidence.

With our in-house team and trusted consultants, we bring design and liaisoning together, coordinating with BMC, MHADA, SRA, MBMC and MMRDA to make the journey smoother and more organised.

With 100+ completed projects across residential, commercial, SRA, township and interior developments, we bring the experience, clarity and commitment needed to help every project move forward.
