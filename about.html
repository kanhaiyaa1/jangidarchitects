About Us

OUR STORY

Who we are

Jangid Architects is an architecture and liaisoning firm based in Malad (East), Mumbai, practising since 2005. For nearly two decades we have designed buildings for developers, housing societies and private owners across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

Design is only half of the work. We also prepare and follow up building plan approvals, including IOD, CC and OC, with BMC, MHADA, SRA, MBMC and MMRDA. With one team handling both, projects lose less time between the drawing board and the approval desk.

Our 70+ completed projects include residential redevelopment, SRA schemes, commercial and mixed-use buildings, townships, industrial work and interiors. Whatever the size, each project gets the same careful detailing and clear communication until handover.
