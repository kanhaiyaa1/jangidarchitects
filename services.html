REGULATORY EXPERTISE

Every authority in the Mumbai MMR

Mumbai has multiple planning authorities operating in parallel, and the approval path for any given site depends entirely on which authority has jurisdiction. We've worked across all of them.

BMC
Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation

Covers Greater Mumbai. All IOD, CC, and OC for Mumbai city and suburbs falls under BMC jurisdiction.

MHADA
Maharashtra Housing & Area Development Authority

Manages MHADA colony redevelopment, lottery housing, and rehabilitation schemes across Maharashtra.

SRA
Slum Rehabilitation Authority

Regulates slum redevelopment schemes: scheme registration, project approvals, transit accommodation, and sale component.

MBMC
Mira-Bhayander Municipal Corporation

Governs development in Mira Road and Bhayander, part of the MMR with its own building permission processes.

MMRDA
Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority

Regional planning authority for the broader MMR. Covers township approvals, special planning areas (SPAs), and major infrastructure projects beyond BMC limits.

02

Building Plan Approvals

Plan approval is the most time-sensitive part of any project. Our approvals team handles submission, follow-up and rectification so you don't have to.

We prepare the documents for BMC, MHADA, SRA, MBMC or MMRDA, whichever has jurisdiction, and liaise with officers until the approval is in hand.

What's included
  • IOD (Intimation of Disapproval) application & follow-up
  • CC (Commencement Certificate): plinth, upper floors, basement
  • OC (Occupancy Certificate) application
  • DP remarks & zoning certificate procurement
  • NA order coordination (where applicable)
  • Documents: title report, architect and structural certificates
  • Authority queries, objections & rectifications

07

Development Permissions

Some projects need permissions beyond plan approval: change of user, land conversion, height or FSI relaxations and environmental clearances, each from a different department.

We prepare, submit and follow up every one, so a missing clearance never holds up your project.

What's included
  • Change of user (CU) permission applications
  • NA (Non-Agricultural) land conversion
  • Height relaxation permissions (aviation, defence)
  • Environmental clearance (EC) for large projects
  • Fire NOC & lift inspector coordination
  • Tree cutting & garden permission (MTDC/BMC)
  • Road-widening & reservation release coordination
  • Special permissions under Section 37/38 of MR&TP Act
