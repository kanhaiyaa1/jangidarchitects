AREAS WE SERVE

Architects and liaisoning consultants in Malad, Mumbai

Jangid Architects works from our office at 302 & 303, Raj Chambers, Manchubhai Road, Malad (East), Mumbai 400 097. For over twenty years we have prepared plans, followed up approvals and supported redevelopment for developers, housing societies and property owners across Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

Every approval file is handled by the office that has jurisdiction over the site. Our team knows the process at BMC, MBMC, MHADA, SRA and MMRDA, and follows each file until the approval is issued.

AUTHORITIES

Authorities we work with

The approval path depends on where the site is. These are the authorities we deal with, and the areas each one covers.

BMC
Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation

Greater Mumbai, including Malad, Borivali, Andheri, Goregaon and the island city. IOD/IOA, CC and OC.

MBMC
Mira-Bhayander Municipal Corporation

Mira Road and Bhayander. Building permission files.

MHADA
Maharashtra Housing & Area Development Authority

MHADA colony redevelopment and lottery housing approvals.

SRA
Slum Rehabilitation Authority

Scheme registration, approvals and transit accommodation for SRA projects.

MMRDA
Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority

Regional planning and township approvals across the MMR.
